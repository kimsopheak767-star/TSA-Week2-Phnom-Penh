# Learning Log

## Week 2 — Phnom Penh Monthly Precipitation (2015–2025)

### What I learned this week
- A **time plot** shows the entire series in chronological order.
- A **seasonal plot** puts month on the x-axis and draws one line per year.
- A **seasonal subseries plot** gives each month its own mini time plot with a mean line.
- Seasonal patterns do NOT mean values must be identical every year.
- An unusual value is NOT automatically a measurement error.

### Key findings from the Phnom Penh data
- **Wet season:** May–October; **dry season:** January–April.
- **Wettest month on average:** September (~227 source units).
- **Driest month on average:** February (~12 source units).
- **Most unusual observation:** June 2020 = 703.62.
- **No clear long-term trend** over the 11 years.
